## Universe Selection

Every strategy has Globals(...) and Universe(...) declarations above the Pipeline (DSL Syntax has Globals).

**Universe** declares which assets to trade. A fixed basket is `Universe(mode="manual", symbols=[...])` and holds exactly those symbols — no resolve step, no `resolved=` to fill in. Criteria modes (`top_volume`, `category`) are resolved server-side when the strategy is saved: every mode carries `resolved=[...]` after a save, written by the platform rather than by hand. After a save, the strategy's universe (mode, resolved count, the list) says what resolved — the list a backtest runs on.

**Universe selectors** (all live in the `Universe(...)` declaration and persist in the DSL alongside `resolved`): `mode` (`manual` | `category` | `top_volume`), `market` (`perp` only — the default), `symbols` (manual mode), `categories` (category mode), `top_n` (top_volume mode), `inclusions` / `exclusions` (force-include / exclude symbols), `lookback` (`7d` | `30d` | `90d` — the volume-ranking window for `top_volume`, default `7d`), `volume_quartiles` (`q1`–`q4`, where q1 = top 25% by volume; filters the volume ranking, alone or combined with `top_n`), `min_trailing_dollar_volume` (a trailing-24h dollar-volume floor; `min_trailing_notional_proxy` is its deprecated alias), and `groups`. Volume here is dollar volume: traded notional, the sum of trade price × size. These match the web editor exactly; they are set in the `Universe(...)` declaration, and a save snapshots the concrete list into `resolved`.

**Resolved universe semantics**: The backtest and deploy path use `Universe.resolved=[...]` as the concrete asset list (baked at save; a manual basket saved without it is read as its `symbols` minus exclusions plus inclusions). `mode='top_volume'`, categories, inclusions, and exclusions are criteria for resolving that list; they are NOT a rolling historical selector during the backtest. Once resolved, the universe stays fixed until it is explicitly changed and resolved again. A resolved `top_volume` universe does not update during the backtest, and it is not lookahead bias. The narrower realism issue is "current resolved universe / survivorship bias"; a new snapshot or a different asset scope comes from refreshing or changing the universe criteria. Its concrete check is a re-run on a manual basket of names already liquid at the window start, compared with the original.

**Market**: Keel trades Hyperliquid perpetual futures only, so `"perp"` is the only valid `market` value and the default — omit `market=` or write `market="perp"`. Spot is not supported: `market="spot"`, or any other value, is refused with `UNSUPPORTED_MARKET`. There is NO `"hl_perp"` market type — when users say "HL perps", "Hyperliquid perps", or "Hyperliquid perpetuals", use `market="perp"`. The platform trades exclusively on Hyperliquid, so `"perp"` already means Hyperliquid perpetual futures.

**Volume filtering**: `top_volume` universes are already filtered to the highest-volume assets on the exchange. Adding `RollingVolumeUniverseMask` to a `top_volume` universe is redundant — these assets already meet liquidity thresholds by definition. Volume filtering applies to a `manual` universe with potentially illiquid assets, or to an explicit request for volume-based filtering. The volume masks read dollar volume from a `dollar_volume_slot` wired from `DollarVolumeLoader() -> Store(...)`.

**Groups**: For multi-group strategies (e.g., 'long DeFi, short L1'):

1. Define groups in Universe: groups={'defi': ['AAVE', 'UNI', ...], 'l1': ['BTC', 'ETH', ...]}
2. In the pipeline, use GroupAssetFilter(group='defi') in each Parallel branch, each branch ending in its sizer
3. Follow with WeightConcatenator to merge branches with different assets (Strategy Patterns, "Directional Pair/Basket")
